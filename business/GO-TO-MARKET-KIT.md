another vertical, swap "clinic" → your niche and adjust the three example numbers._

### 1c. Cadence & engagement (realistic for a solo operator)

- **Post 2×/week.** Rotate the three types: value list → case study/insight → problem/opinion. Reuse the patterns above; you'll never run out.
- **15 min/day of commenting** on posts by local business owners and clinic groups. Thoughtful comments get you more reach than your own posts, early on.
- **Turn connections into conversations** — see the outreach DM below. Connect first, provide value, offer the free pilot only after a reply.
- **The flywheel:** every pilot → one anonymized result → a LinkedIn post + a testimonial on the site + (later) a short article. One piece of work, three uses.

## PART 2 — Outreach Kit

### 2a. Target list (aim for 20 to start)

Track in a simple sheet — one row per prospect:

| Business | Owner | Contact (email / LinkedIn) | Sub-type | Source | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Where to find 20 in an afternoon:**
- **Google Maps** — search "physio clinic [your city]" / "dental practice [city]"; owners' names are often on the site's About page.
- **LinkedIn** — search job title "owner" / "practice manager" + "clinic" + your city.
- **Local directories & the chamber of commerce** member lists.
- **Instagram** — many clinics list a contact email in bio.

Status flow: `To contact → Contacted → Replied → Pilot → Paid → Not now`.

### 2c. Free pilot → paid retainer (the conversion path)

1. **They say yes** → ask for a data export (CSV/Excel from their booking + billing system) or hop on a 15-min screen-share to grab it. Reassure: it stays on your machine.
2. **You deliver** a short report (see checklist below) answering their top 3–5 questions.
3. **The ask** — on the delivery call, end with: *"This is roughly what you'd get every month as an ongoing thing. I do that for [$X]/month — want me to keep it going?"* That's the retainer close. See pricing tiers in the deck/plan.

---

### 2d. "How I run a pilot" — delivery checklist (uses the app as-is, today)

You don't need Electron/licensing for this — you run the analysis in Quarry with your own key.

- [ ] **Scope** — before the data, get their top 3–5 questions ("what do you most wish you knew?").
- [ ] **Get the data** — CSV/Excel export, or screen-share to pull it. Confirm what each column means.
- [ ] **Load it into Quarry** (Open data file → their CSV/Excel).
- [ ] **Analyze** — ask the questions in plain English; let it query + chart.
- [ ] **Verify** — check the audit trail (show the raw rows behind each number) before you trust a figure. Never send a number you haven't sanity-checked.
- [ ] **Export** the report to PowerPoint/Word. Add a short **"What I'd do with this"** — 3 concrete recommendations, not just charts.
- [ ] **Deliver on a 15-min call**, not just an email — walk them through it, then make the retainer offer.
- [ ] **Ask for a testimonial** (and permission to share an anonymized result). That's your next LinkedIn post and site testimonial.

## Adapting to another vertical

Swap "clinic" for your niche and change the three example numbers in Post 3 to that niche's
money levers:
- **Agencies** → revenue by client, utilization/billable hours, client churn.
- **Restaurants** → margin by menu item, covers by day/hour, waste/discount cost.
- **Studios/gyms** → revenue by class type, member retention, no-show rate.

Everything else — the templates, cadence, pilot checklist — carries over unchanged.
