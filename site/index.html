Privacy by design

Your data never leaves your computer

The analysis runs on your machine. There's no server on my side — so there's nothing to leak, lose, or hand over.

✓

Runs on your machine

You open the app and your own files. Nothing is uploaded to me. I literally cannot see your data.

✓

No setup, no AI account

I handle the analyst's brain. No keys, no configuration — you just ask your question.

✓

Sensitive data? Go offline

Flip one switch and even the AI runs locally. Nothing leaves the building — built for health, finance and legal.

The honest version: with cloud AI, only your question and its results reach the AI provider — never me, and never used to train anything. Prefer total isolation? Offline mode sends nothing at all.
